## 3 Present assignment
In consideration of the discharge of the Agreed Debt specified in clause 4, PBK hereby assigns, transfers and conveys to Mikhail all of PBK's existing right, title and interest, if any, in the applications listed in Schedule A and in the inventions disclosed in them, throughout the world, including PBK's associated rights to apply for patents and to claim priority from each listed application under the Paris Convention, the PCT and applicable national law.

The assignment includes PBK's rights in corresponding nonprovisional, PCT, national and regional applications, continuations, divisionals, reissues and resulting patents, to the extent derived from the disclosed inventions and lawfully owned or assignable by PBK. It includes PBK's rights to recover for infringement of the assigned patent rights, to the extent legally transferable. It excludes unrelated inventions, third-party rights and new subject matter not owned by PBK. Additional interests later acquired by PBK in the same disclosed inventions shall be transferred by a further written instrument.

To the extent any inventor rights already belong to Mikhail personally, they remain his. This agreement does not assign those rights from Mikhail to PBK or fabricate an earlier inventor-to-company transfer. Other inventors' rights, if any, require their own valid disposition. The assignment does not change inventorship, extend priority periods or establish support for any future claim.
