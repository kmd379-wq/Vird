PBK ENGINEERING INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS MANAGEMENT – FZCO, a freezone company in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, holding Trade License No. 38769, issues this confirmation regarding the United States provisional patent applications listed in Schedule 1.
Inventors and applicants
Based on the USPTO filing receipts and application records reviewed by the Company, Mikhail Kozin is listed as the inventor and PBK ENGINEERING INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS MANAGEMENT – FZCO is listed as the applicant for each application in Schedule 1. This confirmation does not change the named inventor and does not by itself establish ownership of rights beyond the interests lawfully held by the Company.
Company records regarding the basis of rights
After review of the Company records presently available, the Company confirms the following:
The Company has no separately executed inventor-to-PBK assignment agreement for any of the ten applications.
The Company has no employment agreement, contractor agreement or other written instrument in its records that expressly assigns the patent rights in these applications to PBK.
The Company was named as applicant in the application filings, but the Company does not rely on that designation as a substitute for a separate written assignment where one is required to establish the chain of title.
The Company has not identified any prior assignment, transfer, security interest, lien, mortgage, pledge or licence affecting the patent rights listed in Schedule 1.
The Company is not aware of any third-party ownership claim or encumbrance affecting those rights, except as may be disclosed in a written document later identified and attached to this confirmation.
Purpose and limitations
This letter is intended to accurately record the state of the Company records and to support legal review of the proposed transfer to Mikhail Kozin. It is not a backdated assignment, does not create a historical transfer that did not occur, and does not replace an executed assignment agreement, inventor agreement, corporate approval or USPTO recordation. If a document inconsistent with this confirmation is later identified, the Company will promptly notify the recipient and correct the record.
Corporate approval
The proposed transfer to Mikhail Kozin is a related-party transaction because Mikhail Kozin is the Company’s shareholder and director. The transfer must be approved by a separate corporate resolution and signed by an authorized representative in accordance with the Company’s constitutional documents and applicable law.
Certification
The undersigned certifies that this confirmation has been approved for issuance by PBK and is accurate to the best of the Company’s knowledge after reasonable review of its records.
