DA VINCI ASSOCIATION FOR INVENTOR'S RIGHTS CO-APPLICANT
AGREEMENT

The undersigned individual or legal entity (hereinafter the "Assignor") hereby
assigns a token interest (hereinafter the "Interest") of 0.0000001 Percent in his or
their to-be-filed PCT application entitled [TITLE OF PCT APPLICATION]
(hereinafter the "Patent") to the Da Vinci Association for Inventor's Rights
(hereinafter the "Association") of Chêne-Bougeries (Canton of Geneva),
Switzerland in consideration of the below promises:

   1. The Association agrees to assign back to the Assignor its Interest in the
      Patent for 1 CHF, within 30 days of receipt of a written request from the
      Assignor to do so.

   2. The Association agrees to be named as a co-applicant in the Patent and thus
      give the Assignor the right to be represented before the PCT authorities in
      patent matters by a firm located in Switzerland.

   3. The Association agrees never to take any action to transfer or license its
      Interest to any third party other than the Assignor or the Assignor's
      successors in interest, and that any attempt to do so by any person will
      have the effect of an immediate assignment of such Interest back to the
      Assignor or its successors in interest.

   4. The Assignor consents to the Association being named as second listed co-
      applicant in the Patent, and has been advised that the Patent will publish
      listing both the Assignor and the Association as co-applicants.

   5. The Association agrees that should it become aware of or be sent any notice
      that might cause the Association to cease to exist, the Association must
      immediately inform the Assignor of such notice, the failure to immediately
      inform the Assignor or their successors in interest resulting in an
      immediate contemporaneous assignment of such Interest back to the
      Assignor or its successor in interest.

   6. The Association will perform this service at no cost to the Assignor, but if
      any contribution is voluntarily made to the Association by the Assignor for
      such service, such contribution will be considered as a paid-up lifetime
