Patent Assignment Agreement
This Patent Assignment Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made effective as of __________________, 2026 (the “Effective Date”), by and between PBK Engineering Intellectual Property Rights Management FZCO, a company organized under the laws of __________________, with registration number __________________ and address __________________ (“Assignor”), and Mikhail Kozin, an individual residing at __________________ (“Assignee”).
1. Assignment
For good and valuable consideration, including the payments and expenditures identified in Exhibit B that were paid personally by Assignee for the filing and prosecution of the applications and were not reimbursed by Assignor, Assignor hereby irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers, and conveys to Assignee all right, title, and interest in and to the provisional patent applications listed in Exhibit A, together with: all inventions and subject matter disclosed in those applications; all domestic and foreign priority rights; all continuation, divisional, continuation-in-part, national-stage, PCT, nonprovisional, reissue, reexamination, and substitute applications claiming benefit of them; and all patents, patent applications, registrations, causes of action, and proceeds arising from or relating to them.
2. Further assurances
Assignor authorizes Assignee and Assignee’s patent counsel to execute and record documents reasonably necessary to confirm or record this assignment with the USPTO or another patent office. Assignor shall, upon reasonable request, sign confirmatory documents that accurately reflect this Agreement.
3. Consideration and no reimbursement
The parties acknowledge that the consideration includes Assignee’s personal payment of the amounts shown in Exhibit B, totaling USD 18,593.00 according to the UpCounsel Transactions Report. Assignor states that it did not reimburse those amounts. The parties may attach payment receipts or a separate accounting if requested.
4. Representations
Assignor represents that it is the applicant or recorded owner identified in the relevant application records, to the extent shown by the USPTO records, and that it has authority to execute this Agreement. Assignor makes no representation regarding patentability, validity, enforceability, or freedom to operate. Assignee accepts the rights subject to the application records and applicable law.
5. Governing law and counterparts
This Agreement is governed by the law selected by the parties’ counsel after confirming the parties’ jurisdictions. It may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature to the extent permitted by applicable law.
