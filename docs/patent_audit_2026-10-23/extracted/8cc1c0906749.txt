 A fixture includes a shelf upon which items may be placed . In one implementation , the shelf is supported by four load cells that provide load cell data that may be used to calculate weight data for the load on the shelf . The load cells are mounted underneath a frame , with an upper portion of a load mount extending upward through the frame and engaged to the shelf . The shelf and the frame include stiffeners to increase rigidity , improving the quality of the load cell data . Electronics and a wiring harness are located underneath the frame . In this configuration , assembly of the fixture may be accomplished completely from the underside , simplifying assembly . 22 Claims , 11 Drawing Sheets
