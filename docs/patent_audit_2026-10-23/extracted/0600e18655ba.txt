the applicant's/owner’s activities in connection with the invention for which a patent is sought or has been 
granted. The applicable Privacy Act System of Records Notice for the information collected in this form is 
COMMERCE/PAT-TM-7 Patent Application Files, available in the Federal Register at 78 FR 19243 (March 29,
