## Scope you described

> **Modular Cabinet Platform — PPAs #3, #4, #5, #6, #7**  
> The physical cabinet architecture: reconfigurable chassis, self-leveling, modular bays, sensor-fusion retrieval, tokenized/biometric access, and the thermal/fluid processing modules that plug into it. This covers a fair amount of hardware variety (chassis/leveling vs. thermal/fluid processing), so there's a real chance the examiner issues a **restriction requirement** and we could then elect one invention — but it's still cheaper and cleaner than filing separate applications up front.

We agree with this strategy. Materials below map each PPA to your six requirements. We will send **one complete application at a time** and will not add or change content during your preparation without understanding additional fees apply.

---

## Your six requirements (per application)

1. Rough sketches or drawings of the device/system (hand-drawn or digital is fine)  
2. A diagram showing how the main components connect and interact  
3. A flowchart or step-by-step walkthrough of how the process/method works  
4. A plain-language description of each component and what it does  
5. One or two concrete examples of the invention in use  
6. Anything that makes your version different from existing/similar products  

**Process:** Gather all materials and send **together, once**, per application. **≥2 weeks** prep time per application after receipt. **One application at a time** — we choose the order.

## Family map — what each PPA covers

| PPA | Title (EN) | Hardware / method focus | Restriction note |
|-----|------------|-------------------------|------------------|
| **#3** | Modular Smart Vending Cabinet | Unified chassis, sensor-fusion manual retrieval, smart-glass, sensory/space-yield pricing | **Base hardware + retrieval** |
| **#4** | Smart Retail Cabinet — Tokenized Access & Split Payments | QR token, consent-before-unlock, split settlement, multi-tenant, master–slave, offline ledger | **Access + payments platform** |
| **#5** | Smart Retail Cabinet (rev.) | Motorized auto-leveling, Visual Tracking Layer, ESL/FIFO, cloud biometric roaming | **Leveling + visual metadata** |
| **#6** | Modular Retail System — Adaptive Architecture | Dual-zone climate, induction pre-pay, fluid station, floating bin, AI pricing, abandonment penalty | **Thermal / fluid processing** |
