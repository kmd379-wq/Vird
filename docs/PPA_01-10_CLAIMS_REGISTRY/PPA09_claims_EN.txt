CLAIMSWhat is claimed is:1. A modular autonomous retail platform system comprising: a plurality of autonomous retail modules, each autonomous retail module including one or more product-holding regions and a local module controller; a platform controller in communication with the local module controllers; a consumer application configured to receive customer feedback associated with a product transaction; a supplier interface associated with one or more suppliers; and a feedback processing engine configured to verify that the customer feedback is linked to a verified transaction and to convert the verified customer feedback into an executable technical control output that changes at least one of supplier access, SKU allow-list status, replenishment authorization, slot allocation, sale authorization, reservation eligibility, or pickup release authorization for at least one autonomous retail module.2. The system of claim 1, wherein the customer feedback comprises a customer-provided numerical quality assessment entered through the consumer application after a purchase, reservation, pickup, return, rejection, or complaint event.3. The system of claim 1, wherein the feedback processing engine is configured to disregard or reduce weight of feedback that is not linked to a verified transaction.4. The system of claim 1, wherein the feedback processing engine computes at least one derived metric selected from a supplier quality score, SKU quality score, batch confidence score, module trust score, location-specific trust score, replenishment confidence value, or sale authorization score.5. The system of claim 4, wherein the platform controller removes a SKU from an allow-list when the SKU quality score or batch confidence score falls below a threshold.6. The system of claim 4, wherein the platform controller denies replenishment authorization to a supplier when the supplier quality score falls below a threshold or when a quality event is associated with a product supplied by the supplier.7. The system of claim 1, further comprising a supplier-to-slot assignment engine configured to assign a supplier or SKU to a slot or shelf zone within an autonomous retail module based at least in part on verified customer feedback, a quality-event metric, demand, turnover, complaint frequency, quality score, or location-specific behavior.8. The system of claim 7, wherein the supplier-to-slot assignment engine changes a slot permission and the local module controller enforces the changed slot permission by enabling or disabling access to a shelf zone, slot, door, compartment, electronic display, product database state, or sale authorization state.9. The system of claim 1, further comprising a reservation module configured to reserve an in-stock product at a selected autonomous retail module for a defined time window and to prevent reservation of a last remaining unit of the in-stock product.10. The system of claim 9, wherein the reservation module prevents reservation of a product when a SKU, batch, supplier, or slot associated with the product is in a restricted quality state.11. The system of claim 1, further comprising a remote-order pickup module configured to accept a remote order for delivery of a product into a selected autonomous retail module, assign a slot or compartment for the product, create a pickup entitlement associated with a consumer identity, and control release of the product based on the pickup entitlement.12. The system of claim 11, wherein the platform controller suspends pickup release when a quality event associated with the ordered product, SKU, supplier, batch, or compartment occurs before pickup.13. The system of claim 1, wherein the executable technical control output comprises at least one command that causes a local module controller to lock a door, disable a compartment, block checkout, change an electronic shelf display, hide a product from the consumer application, prevent reservation, deny replenishment access, or suspend pickup release.14. The system of claim 1, further comprising a dynamic pricing engine configured to adjust a price based on at least one of a customer-provided numerical rating, verified complaint, system-generated quality indicator, stock age, demand, location, reservation demand, pickup demand, quality score, or product availability state.15. The system of claim 1, further comprising a split-payment engine configured to allocate transaction proceeds among at least a supplier and a platform operator according to an inventory ownership mode selected from supplier-owned inventory mode, operator-owned inventory mode, consignment mode, agency mode, or hybrid mode.16. The system of claim 15, wherein the split-payment engine withholds, reduces, reserves, offsets, or suspends a supplier payment based on a verified complaint, customer rating, quality event, refund event, or restricted supplier status.17. A method of controlling a distributed autonomous retail platform, comprising: receiving customer feedback through a consumer application; determining that the customer feedback is linked to a verified transaction involving a product in an autonomous retail module; computing a quality-related metric from the customer feedback; generating a technical control output based on the quality-related metric; transmitting the technical control output to at least one local module controller; and enforcing the technical control output at the autonomous retail module to change at least one of sale authorization, reservation eligibility, pickup release authorization, replenishment authorization, SKU allow-list status, supplier access, or slot allocation.18. The method of claim 17, further comprising receiving system-generated quality data from a sensor of the autonomous retail module and computing the quality-related metric from both the customer feedback and the system-generated quality data.19. The method of claim 17, further comprising denying a reservation request for a last remaining unit of a product while maintaining sale authorization for in-person purchase of the last remaining unit.20. The method of claim 17, further comprising accepting a remote order for a product, assigning the product to a pickup compartment of a selected autonomous retail module, creating a pickup entitlement linked to a consumer account, and blocking pickup release when a quality restriction is associated with the product before pickup.21. The system of claim 1, wherein the platform controller is configured to present, through a display associated with an autonomous retail module, transaction terms indicating that removal of a product from a shelf, cabinet, drawer, slot, bin, tray, hook, pusher, or compartment constitutes acceptance of purchase and that the product is non-returnable, to receive an affirmative user consent input before enabling access to the product, and, after detecting removal of the product using one or more sensors, to automatically charge a payment instrument, increment an authorization hold, or record a purchase commitment associated with the removed product.22. The system of claim 1, wherein the local module controller is configured to detect an attempted return of a removed product based on at least one of a load-cell weight increase, RFID tag reappearance, optical beam event, camera-detected return gesture, or sensor-fusion output, and in response to the attempted return, maintain the original purchase charge, apply a penalty charge, restrict a future access session, or generate an audit record.
