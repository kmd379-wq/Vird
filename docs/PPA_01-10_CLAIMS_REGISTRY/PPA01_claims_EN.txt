ClaimsAn autonomous retail system comprising:a secure enclosure having at least one door;an access-control mechanism configured to lock and unlock the door;at least one smart shelf within the enclosure including a near-field radio-frequency identification (RFID) antenna and an indicator light associated with product positions;a biometric interface including a camera configured to capture facial images of a user;a user interface configured to receive user input and display transaction information; anda controller coupled to the RFID antenna, the biometric interface, the user interface, and the access-control mechanism, the controller being programmed to: (i) maintain inventory data associating products on the smart shelf with identifiers obtained via the RFID antenna; (ii) for perishable products, obtain expiration data, compute dynamic prices based at least in part on the expiration data and sales history, and control the indicator light to visually identify discounted products; (iii) in a restricted-goods mode, receive facial image data, determine that the user satisfies a legal age requirement, request a pre-authorization deposit on a payment instrument associated with the user, and unlock the door in response to approval of the pre-authorization; and (iv) detect, using the RFID antenna, removal of at least one restricted product while the door is unlocked, determine a transaction amount for the removed product, capture the transaction amount from the pre-authorization deposit, and release any remaining portion of the deposit.The system of claim 1, wherein computing dynamic prices comprises applying a discount function that increases a discount as a time remaining until the expiration date decreases, subject to a minimum price threshold.The system of claim 1, wherein controlling the addressable light-emitting diodes comprises illuminating, in a first color or pattern, a zone corresponding to a perishable product whose price has been reduced.The system of claim 1, wherein the processing system is configured to operate in a food mode in which the biometric interface and pre-authorization request are bypassed and the access-control mechanism is configured to unlock based on a payment authorization or remain unlocked, and to operate in the restricted-goods mode in which the biometric interface and pre-authorization request are required before unlocking.The system of claim 1, wherein the deposit amount is determined based on a risk profile associated with the user, the risk profile being a function of at least one of prior transaction history, chargeback history, or product category.The system of claim 1, wherein determining that the user satisfies the legal age requirement comprises estimating an age from the facial image data and comparing the estimated age and a confidence measure to configured thresholds.The system of claim 1, wherein determining that the user satisfies the legal age requirement comprises matching the facial image data to a stored user profile having an age attribute that has been verified via a know-your-customer process.The system of claim 1, wherein the processing system is configured to synchronize inventory data and pricing parameters with a remote server and to receive updated pricing policies or risk profiles from the remote server.A method of operating an autonomous retail system, the method comprising: detecting, using a biometric interface, a user in proximity to a secure enclosure containing restricted products; capturing facial image data of the user; determining, using an age-verification model executed by an edge processing unit, that the user satisfies a legal age requirement; initiating a pre-authorization deposit on a payment instrument associated with the user; upon receiving confirmation of the pre-authorization deposit, unlocking a door of the secure enclosure; detecting, using a matrix of near-field radio-frequency identification antennas, removal of at least one restricted product from a smart shelf; determining a transaction amount corresponding to the removed restricted product; and capturing the transaction amount from the pre-authorization deposit and releasing any remaining portion of the deposit.The method of claim 9 further comprising, for perishable non-restricted products stored on the smart shelves, obtaining expiration data and sales-velocity data, computing dynamic prices based on the expiration data and sales-velocity data, and illuminating addressable light-emitting diodes adjacent discounted products.
