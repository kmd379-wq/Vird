CLAIMSWhat is claimed is:1. A bulk-storage vending machine comprising: a storage hopper configured to store loose perishable products; a disposal bin positioned in a customer-accessible region; a disposal load cell configured to measure a weight of each product placed into the disposal bin; a hopper weighing module configured to measure a hopper load weight; a display; a payment terminal; a central controller configured to maintain a disposal counter and a disposal weight register for a transaction; and an AI pricing module configured to calculate a spoilage index from disposal weight data and to update a displayed price based on the spoilage index.2. The bulk-storage vending machine of claim 1, wherein the central controller is configured to permit a customer to replace up to N disposed products without charge during the transaction and to classify a disposal event exceeding N as an abuse condition.3. The bulk-storage vending machine of claim 2, wherein, in response to the abuse condition, the central controller is configured to add a charge for at least one disposed product to the transaction through the payment terminal.4. The bulk-storage vending machine of claim 1, wherein the disposal weight register increases when a product is placed into the disposal bin while an accounting weight of the hopper load is not decreased by the disposal event.5. The bulk-storage vending machine of claim 1, wherein the AI pricing module calculates the spoilage index as a ratio of cumulative disposal weight to an initial or current hopper load weight.6. The bulk-storage vending machine of claim 1, wherein the AI pricing module is configured to reduce the displayed price when the spoilage index exceeds a threshold.7. The bulk-storage vending machine of claim 1, wherein the storage hopper comprises a conical hopper bottom configured to direct loose perishable products toward a central weighing region by gravity.8. The bulk-storage vending machine of claim 7, wherein the conical hopper bottom has an angle of about 45 degrees relative to horizontal.9. The bulk-storage vending machine of claim 7, wherein the conical hopper bottom has an angle between about 35 degrees and about 60 degrees relative to horizontal.10. The bulk-storage vending machine of claim 7, wherein the hopper weighing module comprises a single central load cell positioned at or below the central weighing region.11. The bulk-storage vending machine of claim 10, wherein the conical hopper bottom and the single central load cell are configured to reduce corner-load error, sidewall-loading error, or calibration complexity relative to a multi-load-cell hopper platform.12. The bulk-storage vending machine of claim 1, wherein the storage hopper comprises an automatic product-leveling system positioned within the storage hopper and configured to redistribute products within the hopper.13. The bulk-storage vending machine of claim 12, wherein the automatic product-leveling system comprises at least one of a motorized sweep arm, rotating paddle, agitator, vibration actuator, oscillating plate, air-pulse nozzle, auger, movable floor panel, tilting baffle, flexible wiper, product rake, or servo-driven leveling blade.14. The bulk-storage vending machine of claim 12, wherein the central controller activates the automatic product-leveling system in response to at least one of load-cell instability, image-detected mounding, product bridging, sidewall bias, hopper-level sensor imbalance, blocked flow, or a dispensing error.15. The bulk-storage vending machine of claim 12, wherein the central controller pauses or filters load-cell measurements during operation of the automatic product-leveling system and resumes weighing after a settling interval or weight-stability condition is satisfied.16. The bulk-storage vending machine of claim 1, wherein the disposal bin comprises at least one of a motorized lid, one-way slot, valve, status indicator, camera, RFID reader, or retrieval-prevention structure.17. The bulk-storage vending machine of claim 1, wherein the display is configured to show a current unit price, a disposed-item weight for the transaction, and a transaction status selected from replacement available, limit reached, or abuse charge applied.18. The bulk-storage vending machine of claim 1, further comprising an imaging sensor configured to detect spoilage signs or product distribution within the storage hopper, wherein the AI pricing module further uses imaging data to adjust a price or generate an operator notification.19. A method of operating a bulk-storage vending machine, comprising: initiating a customer transaction; resetting a disposal counter; allowing a customer to inspect a product from a storage hopper; measuring, by a disposal load cell, a weight of a product placed into a disposal bin; incrementing the disposal counter; determining whether the disposal counter exceeds a threshold N; increasing a disposal weight register without decreasing an accounting weight of the hopper load; calculating a spoilage index from the disposal weight register and hopper load weight; and updating a displayed price based on the spoilage index.20. The method of claim 19, further comprising directing loose products in the storage hopper toward a single central load cell using a conical hopper bottom having an angle of about 45 degrees relative to horizontal and redistributing the loose products using an automatic product-leveling system positioned within the storage hopper.
