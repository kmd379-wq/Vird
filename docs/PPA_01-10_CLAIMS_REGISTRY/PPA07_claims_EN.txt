CLAIMSWhat is claimed is:An Autonomous Retail Ecosystem comprising:A reconfigurable master cabinet chassis having a universal power distributioninterface selected from a vertical busbar, a cable chain, or a suspended loop;a modular shelving system supporting interchangeable modules; a distributedsensor network; and an edge computing controller;Wherein the system further comprises:(a) A Hybrid Induction Heating Module configured as a hermeticallysealable chamber to store items at a chilled temperature and performon-demand heating using an induction coil interacting with a susceptor-based packaging layer, controlled by an RFID cooking profile;(b) An Active Negative-Pressure Ventilation System configured to evacuatesteam and odors directly from the chamber during the heating cycle toprevent condensation in the chilled zone;(c) An Automated Sanitation Purge Mechanism configured to mechanicallydivert a heated item into a Sub-Zero Waste Module (maintained below -2°C) if not retrieved within a timeout period, thereby arresting bacterialgrowth and triggering a transactional penalty;(d) A Mechanical Anti-Reverse System comprising ratchets on productpushers that physically prevent the return of dispensed items; and(e) A Smart Glass Interface (PDLC) configured to transition from an opaquestate to a transparent inspection state only upon biometric verification.The System of Claim 1 (Produce & Weighing):Further comprising a Secure Open-Zone Produce Station characterized by:(i) Conical Hopper Scales featuring a specific 45-degreegeometry to gravitationally direct loose items to a singlecentral load cell, thereby eliminating corner-load errorswithout requiring multi-sensor arrays;(ii) Active Auto-Leveling Feet comprising motorized actuators andgyroscopic sensors configured to physically level the chassisto ensure gravimetric accuracy;(iii) A Shrinkage Compensation Engine configured to adjust unitpricing exponentially based on real-time moisture loss; and(iv) A Total-Sum Verification Gateway that tracks the cumulativeweight of all dispensed items (loose and packaged) andactuates an exit barrier only upon confirming a match with thetotal weight measured at the exit platform.The System of Claim 1 (Physical AI Pricing):Wherein the controller executes a localized Space-Yield Displacement Engineconfigured to physically optimize cabinet inventory allocation by adjustingunit prices based on a weighted aggregate of:(i) Optical and Gravimetric Sensory Decay: Real-time correlationof surface texture analysis (via cameras) and moisture weightloss (via scales);(ii) Spatial Opportunity Cost: A calculated metric representing therevenue potential of the physical volume occupied by a slow-moving item versus a high-velocity alternative;(iii) Contextual Demand Fluidity: Real-time adjustment factorsderived from local time-of-day, weather conditions, andproximity-based competitor pricing data;Thereby inducing a localized accelerated sales velocity to physically clearshelf space.The System of Claim 1 (Ecosystem & Inter-Device):Further comprising a Smart Cooler Synchronization protocol, wherein thecabinet transmits a dispensing command to a proximate water dispenser torelease a precise volume of water (e.g., 350ml) corresponding to a specificSKU purchased in the cabinet, and a Smart Cutlery Dispenser that releases aspecific utensil (fork/spoon/chopstick) matched to the product type.The System of Claim 1 (Supplier Logic):Further comprising a Partitioned Multi-Tenant Control Architecture, allowingdistinct supplier entities to independently control pricing of their respectiveinventory slots via a remote application, utilizing an Instant Split-PaymentSmart Contract that divides transaction revenue between the platformoperator and the supplier in real-time.
