                            CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT
This Confidentiality Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date of the last signature below (the
"Effective Date") by and among (1) Mikhail Kozin, an individual and citizen of the Russian Federation
residing at Flat 503, Plot 70, Sharjah Main City, United Arab Emirates ("Kozin"); (2) PBK
ENGINEERING INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS MANAGEMENT - FZCO, a free-zone
company licensed by the Dubai Integrated Economic Zones Authority under License No. 38769, with its
registered office at IFZA Business Park, DDP, P.O. Box 342001, Dubai, United Arab Emirates ("PBK";
Kozin and PBK each a "Discloser" and together the "Disclosers"); and (3) Michael Bartholomew,
Attorney at Law, Bartholomew Law PC, 5842 W Timber Ridge Lane, Highland, Utah 84003, United
States of America ("Recipient").

A. Purpose.
Recipient desires to receive certain Confidential Information from the Disclosers solely for the purpose of
(i) evaluating the Disclosers' patent portfolio and related ownership documents in order to provide a scope
and fee assessment, and (ii) if the parties subsequently enter into a written engagement agreement,
performing the legal services described in that engagement, including the preparation, filing, recordation
and prosecution of patent applications and related instruments before the United States Patent and
Trademark Office ("USPTO"), the World Intellectual Property Organization ("WIPO") and other patent
Offices (together, the "Purpose"). Recipient will not use the Confidential Information for any other purpose.
This Agreement does not itself create an attorney-client engagement; any engagement will be set out in a
separate written agreement.

B. Definition of Confidential Information.
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of either Discloser
to Recipient (whether disclosed before or after the Effective Date, including the review package transmitted
in five parts in October 2026), in any form (oral, written, visual, electronic, tangible or otherwise), including
without limitation: unpublished patent applications and their specifications, claims, drawings, filing records
and correspondence; inventions (patentable or not); business, financial, product, technical, operational,
customer/vendor, pricing, marketing, plans/roadmaps, designs, source code/object code, know-how, data,
processes and trade secrets; corporate, ownership and settlement documents; and any notes, summaries,
analyses, compilations or other materials prepared by Recipient that contain or reflect such information.
Confidential Information includes information that (i) is marked or designated confidential, (ii) is disclosed
under circumstances reasonably indicating it is confidential, or (iii) by its nature should reasonably be
understood to be confidential. Information disclosed by either Discloser is Confidential Information of both
Disclosers, and either Discloser may enforce this Agreement.

1. Use Restrictions.
Recipient shall use Confidential Information only for the Purpose and for no other purpose, including
without limitation for Recipient's own benefit or any competitive or commercial use. Recipient shall not
reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble or otherwise attempt to derive the composition, underlying ideas,
algorithms, structure or operation of any prototypes, samples, software, models or other materials provided
as Confidential Information, except to the extent necessary for the Purpose or expressly authorized in
writing by a Discloser.

2. Non-Disclosure; Standard of Care.
Recipient shall protect and keep confidential all Confidential Information and shall not disclose it to any
third party except as permitted under Paragraphs 3, 4 and 6. Recipient shall use at least the same degree of
care it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar importance, and in any event no less than
the care required of a lawyer with respect to client confidences under the rules of professional conduct
applicable to Recipient.

3. Permitted Disclosures to Representatives.
(a) Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its employees, contract staff and professional
advisors (together, "Representatives") who (i) have a bona fide need to know for the Purpose, and (ii) are
bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement. Recipient is
responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

(b) Recipient shall not disclose Confidential Information to any outside associate, including any foreign
patent agent or attorney, patent drawing service, translator or search provider ("Outside Associate"), unless
Kozin has first approved in writing (email is sufficient) the identity of the Outside Associate and the scope
of the disclosure. Each approved Outside Associate shall be bound by written confidentiality obligations at
least as protective as this Agreement before any disclosure is made.

4. Professional Obligations; Authorized Filings.
(a) This Agreement supplements, and does not limit, Recipient's duties of confidentiality to the Disclosers
as a prospective or actual client under the rules of professional conduct applicable to Recipient, which
continue to apply regardless of the term of this Agreement.

(b) Disclosures made by Recipient to the USPTO, WIPO, any receiving Office, International Searching
Authority or other patent Office, or to a court or governmental authority, in the course of preparing, filing,
recording or prosecuting applications or instruments that a Discloser has authorized in writing, including
disclosures required by Recipient's duty of candor and good faith to those Offices, are not breaches of this
Agreement.

5. Exclusions.
Recipient's obligations under this Agreement do not apply to information that Recipient can prove by
competent written records: (a) is or becomes generally available to the public through no breach of this
Agreement by Recipient or its Representatives (including by publication of a patent application by a patent
Office); (b) was lawfully known to Recipient without restriction before disclosure by a Discloser; (c) is
rightfully received by Recipient from a third party without breach of any duty of confidentiality owed to a
Discloser; or (d) was independently developed by Recipient without use of or reference to the Disclosers'
Confidential Information.

6. Compelled Disclosure.
If Recipient (or a Representative) is required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order or governmental
demand to disclose any Confidential Information, Recipient shall (to the extent legally permitted) provide
the Disclosers prompt written notice and reasonably cooperate (at the Disclosers' expense) in seeking a
protective order or other remedy. If disclosure is nonetheless required, Recipient will disclose only the
portion legally required and will use reasonable efforts to ensure confidential treatment.

7. No License; Ownership.
All Confidential Information remains the exclusive property of the Discloser that owns it. Nothing in this
Agreement grants Recipient any rights, by license or otherwise, in or to the Disclosers' Confidential
Information or intellectual property, except the limited right to use the Confidential Information solely for
the Purpose. Nothing in this Agreement determines or affects ownership of any patent application or
invention as between Kozin and PBK.

8. No Obligation; No Warranty.
Nothing in this Agreement obligates any party to proceed with any engagement, transaction or relationship,
and receipt of Confidential Information does not authorize Recipient to undertake billable work, filings or
recordations absent a separate written engagement. All Confidential Information is provided "AS IS"
without any representation or warranty, express or implied, including as to accuracy or completeness.

9. Return or Destruction.
Upon a Discloser's written request, or upon termination of discussions without an engagement, Recipient
shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information (including all copies and derivative materials)
and certify in writing upon request that it has done so; provided that Recipient may retain (i) one archival
copy and (ii) such copies as are required to be kept under the record-retention, trust-accounting and
professional-conduct rules applicable to Recipient, in each case subject to the continuing confidentiality
obligations of this Agreement.

10. Term; Survival.
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date. Recipient's confidentiality and restricted-use obligations
continue for five (5) years from the last disclosure of Confidential Information; however, as to information
qualifying as a trade secret under applicable law, such obligations continue for so long as the information
remains a trade secret, and Recipient's professional duties under Paragraph 4(a) continue for as long as
those rules require.

11. Remedies.
Recipient acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information may cause
irreparable harm for which monetary damages may be inadequate. Each Discloser is entitled to seek
injunctive or equitable relief (without posting bond or proving actual damages where permitted), in addition
to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

12. Governing Law; Venue.
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Utah, United States of America, without regard to
its conflict-of-laws rules. The parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction and venue of the state and federal
courts located in Utah County, Utah, for any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement, provided
that a Discloser may seek injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

13. Assignment.
Recipient may not assign or transfer this Agreement (by operation of law or otherwise) without the
Disclosers' prior written consent, and any attempted assignment in violation of this Paragraph is void. Either
Discloser may assign this Agreement to a successor in interest to the Confidential Information, including
to the other Discloser.

14. Severability.
If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full
force and effect, and the invalid provision will be enforced to the maximum extent permitted.

15. Entire Agreement; Amendment; Counterparts; Language.
This Agreement is the entire agreement among the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all
prior or contemporaneous understandings on that subject; it does not supersede any engagement agreement
subsequently signed, which will govern the legal services. Any amendment must be in writing and signed
by all parties. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which
is deemed an original. This Agreement is made in English; any translation is for convenience only and the
English text controls.
