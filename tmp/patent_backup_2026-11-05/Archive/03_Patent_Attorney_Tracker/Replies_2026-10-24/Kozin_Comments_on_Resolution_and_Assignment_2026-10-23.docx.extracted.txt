A. Overall assessment
Both drafts are well constructed and, with the edits below, the assignment will be
suitable for electronic recordation against all ten provisional applications through
USPTO Assignment Center. The main substantive point is sequencing: the assignment
must be fully signed (in both capacities) before any PCT application is filed, because the
right to claim priority passes with it, and before PBK’s trade license expires on December
10, 2026. I recommend signing no later than November 13, 2026.

The 51-page Memorandum and Articles of Association (Zoho-signed; DIEZ Implementing
Regulations 2023) is the operative version. It contains Articles 43, 60, 66 and 69 as cited in
the resolution. The “alternate version” Memorandum cites 2006 regulations, lacks those
Articles and carries no signatures; it should not be relied on.

D. Recordation mechanics
One electronic cover sheet (Assignment Center) will list all ten applications as properties,
conveyance type “Assignment of assignors interest,” assignor PBK ENGINEERING
INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS MANAGEMENT - FZCO, assignee Mikhail Kozin, with
the signed English agreement (without the settlement letter) as the supporting document.
Official fee for electronic recording: USD 0. The Notice of Recordation with reel and
frame numbers typically issues within a few business days. A power of attorney in the
provisionals is not needed for recordation, but it is needed to download the as-filed
documents from Patent Center and to obtain the DAS access codes for the PCT priority
documents; I will prepare that form for PBK’s signature.
